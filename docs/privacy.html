Privacy Policy

Morse Than Words · Effective 6 October 2026

Morse Than Words does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (speed, tone, radio conditions, practice preferences).
  • Your practice history (date, character set, speed and the characters that were sent).

This information is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere, not even to iCloud. You can delete your practice history in the app (Practice › History), and deleting the app removes everything.

Text you type, paste or import to send as Morse is kept only while the app is open; it is not saved, and imported files are only read, never changed or copied.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
