Frequently asked questions

I can't hear anything.
Check the volume in Settings › Volume and your device volume. The app plays even with the silent switch on. Audio stops when the app goes to the background.
What do WPM and Farnsworth mean?
Speed is in words per minute using the standard word "PARIS". Farnsworth spacing sends each character at full speed but adds longer pauses between characters and words, which makes learning by sound easier.
How do I enter prosigns?
Type them in angle brackets: <AR>, <SK>, <BT>, <KN> or <SOS>.
Which text files can I import?
Plain-text (.txt) files from the Files app, in UTF-8, UTF-16 or Latin-1/Windows-1252. Up to 100,000 characters are sent.
Where is my practice history stored?
Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
