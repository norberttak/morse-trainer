### Description (≤ 4000)

<!-- description -->
Morse Than Words teaches you to hear Morse code, the way radio operators learn it: by sound.

LEARN
Tap any letter, number, punctuation mark or prosign to hear it, and see its code as dots and dashes.

PRACTICE RECEIVING
Pick the characters you want to train — start with the first Koch-method characters, or choose letters, numbers or your own set. Choose how many characters you want to hear and how they are grouped. Write what you hear on paper, then reveal what was sent and compare. Every completed session is kept in your history on the device.

SEND ANY TEXT
Type or paste text, or import a .txt file, and listen to it in Morse while a ticker shows the current character — or hide the text and copy by ear.

REAL RADIO CONDITIONS
Make it sound like a shortwave radio: band noise, slow fading (QSB), static crashes (QRN), a receiver filter and distortion.

YOUR SPEED, YOUR TONE
• Character speed from 5 to 50 words per minute
• Farnsworth spacing for easier learning
• Tone pitch from 300 to 1200 Hz, volume and click-free keying

FREE AND PRIVATE
No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. The app never connects to the internet. Your settings and practice history stay on your device.

Works on iPhone and iPad, in light and dark mode, with Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.
<!-- /description -->
